MAKE THE SMALL STUFF USEFUL

Turn leftover tokens into usable value

Tidify identifies empty token accounts and scattered Solana token balances — dust left over from swaps, airdrops, and protocol claims — estimates what each one is worth, and lets you swap, burn, or close the accounts you choose. Everything is non-custodial: your wallet signs, and nothing is forced.

How it works

  1. Connect or paste a wallet. Tidify reads the wallet's token accounts on Solana mainnet — no signature needed to preview.
  2. Scan and price. Every balance is priced in USD; each token gets a friendly name from on-chain metadata when available.
  3. Review the plan. For each position Tidify proposes the economical action — swap, burn, or close — with estimated recovery, fees, and rent refund.
  4. Approve one batch. You check every account and sign a real mainnet transaction batch. Recovered value lands directly in your wallet.

Safety

  • Non-custodial. Funds never pass through a Tidify-controlled account; your wallet signs every transaction.
  • Nothing is forced. Every account must be explicitly selected; the default is always to do nothing.
  • Fee ceiling on-chain. The protocol fee is configurable on-chain and capped; the preview shows the maximum before you approve.
  • Unverified tokens are labeled. Tokens without verifiable metadata are marked "Unverified token" and link to an explorer so you know exactly what you are looking at.
  • Unsupported tokens are left alone. Non-transferable or unsupported token accounts are never swapped.

Fees

Tidify charges a small protocol fee, set on-chain and capped, on the value it helps you recover. You also pay the Solana network fee and, when a swap route is used, the Jupiter platform fee. Every position shows its estimated net recovery before you approve anything — no surprises at signing time.

FAQ

What is token dust?

Small leftover SPL Token or Token-2022 balances that sit in a Solana wallet after swaps, airdrops, or protocol claims. Each one is too small to use but still occupies an account that holds rent.

Does Tidify take custody of my funds?

No. Tidify is non-custodial: your wallet signs every transaction, funds never pass through a Tidify-controlled account, and you explicitly approve each position before anything is sent.

Do I need to connect a wallet to see what I can recover?

No. Paste any Solana address in the guest preview to see an estimate of recoverable value, the proposed close/burn/swap plan, and fees — before connecting anything.

Which tokens are supported?

Standard SPL Token and Token-2022 accounts. Non-transferable, unsupported, or unverified tokens are never swapped — they are clearly labeled and left untouched unless you choose to reclaim their rent by closing the account.

What does it cost?

A small protocol fee that is set on-chain, capped, and shown in the preview before you approve; the Solana network fee; and the Jupiter swap platform fee when a swap route is used. The estimated net recovery is always shown per position.

Which network does Tidify run on?

Solana mainnet. The Tidify program and its fee configuration live on-chain, and every action is a normal, visible Solana transaction.
